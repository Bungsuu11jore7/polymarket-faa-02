Polymarket FAA Attribution Forecaster

A stdlib-only, offline, deterministic Python forecaster for Polymarket's
FAA outage attribution prices: for every market and every minute of the 4
hours after each checkpoint, five quantiles of that minute's Yes price.

Method: per-market empirical volatility (RMS of 1-minute price changes over
the last 120 minutes, floored and capped), persistence median at the
checkpoint price, spread growing as h**0.35, outer quantiles widened x1.3
for jump-aware tails. No drift, no mean reversion — both hurt the backtest.

Practice backtest (6 cases p00-p05, mean pinball loss x100):
house baseline 0.363589, this forecaster 0.269237 (-26%), better on every case.
